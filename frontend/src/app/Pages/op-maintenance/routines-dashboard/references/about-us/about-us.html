About Operational Maintenance

“Timely Maintenance is essential to have reduced equipment failure rate”

Routines tracking onboard IN Ships is an essential and time-intensive task. Details often need to be manually retrieved from MAINTOPS and logbooks, causing delays and inefficiencies...

Operational Maintenance simplifies this by providing instant visibility of all routines falling due in the next six months or 1000 hours, enabling better planning and resource allocation.
