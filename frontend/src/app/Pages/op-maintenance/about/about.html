Effortless Routines Tracking Onboard IN Ships

"Timely Maintenance is essential to have reduced equipment failure rate."

Operation Maintenance replaces manual MAINTOP register and logbook look-ups with a single digital source of truth for equipment routines, defects and running-hour tracking across the ship.

It gives every department instant visibility of routines due in the next 6 months or the next 1000 running hours, so maintenance can be planned ahead rather than discovered after the fact.
