"Timely maintenance ensures operational readiness and mission success."

Ship Returns is a digital module designed to simplify the monitoring and submission of operational and maintenance returns from ships of the Indian Navy. Traditionally, ship staff had to manually compile routine reports from logbooks and maintenance systems such as MAINTOPS, which required significant time and effort.

The Returns Module provides a centralized platform where ships can initiate, track, and submit periodic returns including monthly, quarterly, and annual reports. The system ensures accurate record keeping, better visibility of equipment status, and timely reporting to higher command authorities.

By digitizing these processes, the platform reduces administrative workload onboard ships and improves efficiency, transparency, and operational readiness across the naval fleet.

Built at Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited, Mumbai, INS Kochi represents India's growing capability in indigenous defence shipbuilding and naval technology.
