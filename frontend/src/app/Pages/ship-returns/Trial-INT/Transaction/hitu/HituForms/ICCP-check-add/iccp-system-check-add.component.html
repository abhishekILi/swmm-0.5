Check No.7

To check ICCP system functionality and to record amount of current required to be impressed to maintain the hull potential in the protected zone
  1. 1. ACU-1 switched ON in manual mode. Other ACU(s) switched OFF.
  2. 2. Current to be impressed in increments of 01 or 02 Amps.
  3. 3. Check response to increase of impressed current by means of ICCP REs associated with ACU-1. Cross check with Portable REs.
  4. 4. Increase currents till the point where the hull potential starts to decrease (in the case of ships with hull potential in the protected/over-protected zone).
  5. 5. Increase currents till hull potential is within the protected zone ie. 220–250mV (in the case of ships with hull potential in under protected zone).
