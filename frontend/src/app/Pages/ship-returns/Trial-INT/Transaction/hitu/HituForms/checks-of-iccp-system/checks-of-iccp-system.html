Check No. 7 : ACU NO. 1

Check No. 7
To check ICCP system functionality and to record amount of current required to be impressed to maintain the hull potential in the protected zone
  1. ACU-1 switched ON in manual mode. Other ACU(s) switched OFF.
  2. Current to be impressed in increments of 01 or 02 Amps.
  3. Check response to increase of impressed current by means of ICCP REs associated with ACU-1. Cross check with Portable REs.
  4. Increase currents till the point where the hull potential starts to decrease (in the case of ships with hull potential in the protected/over-protected zone).
  5. Increase currents till hull potential is within the protected zone ie. 220-250mV (in the case of ships with hull potential in under protected zone)

Check No. 8 : ACU NO. 1

Check No. 8
To check ICCP system functionality in ‘auto’ mode and to record amount of current impressed to maintain the hull potential iaw the set potential
  1. ACU1 switched ON in ‘auto’ mode.
  2. Other ACUs to be switched OFF.
  3. If hull potential with ACUs-OFF is in protected/ over protected zone and lower than the preset potential, then start the check with the ACU at the preset potential. Reduce hull potential in small increments till the system starts to impress current and the hull potential starts to reduce further.
  4. If the hull potential with ACUs-OFF is in the under protected zone, then start the check with the ACU preset potential set to 270mV (zinc RE) or -780mV (Ag-AgCl RE).
  5. Reduce hull potential in small increments till one step lower than the preset potential
