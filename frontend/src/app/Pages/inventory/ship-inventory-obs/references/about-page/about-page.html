About Ship Inventory

"One of the great responsibilities that I have is to manage my assets wisely, so that they create value."

— Alice Walton

The spares held onboard IN Ships reduce the consequences of equipment downtime and play an important role in achieving the desired equipment availability at sea and harbour. Efficient management of Onboard Spares (OBS) is essential — this application can be installed on any warship of the Indian Navy for utilisation by all spare-holding departments.

The key feature of this application is its initial data-import from existing Excel sheets onboard IN ships for instantly populating the database, reducing the initial data-entry effort and human error. Maintaining a critical spares list and handling D787J amendments are among the other highlights of this application.

Ship Inventory serves as the spares utility information repository onboard IN ships throughout the life cycle of a ship, helping expedite DI/DR of fitted equipment by providing historical usage information and making spares retrieval an easy affair.
